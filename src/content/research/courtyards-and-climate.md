## Method

Measurements were taken in eight houses over two summers, recording air temperature, humidity and air speed in the courtyard, the main living room and the street.

## Findings

Courtyards with a height-to-width ratio between 1 and 2 stayed noticeably cooler in the afternoon than the street outside, especially when shaded by trees or screens. Very deep, narrow courtyards trapped warm air at night.

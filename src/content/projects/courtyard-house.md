The house sits on a narrow plot in a dense neighbourhood. Instead of filling the site from edge to edge, the rooms wrap around a planted courtyard that brings light, air and a little privacy into the centre of family life.

Local brick is used throughout — laid solid where walls carry the floors, and laid open as a screen where the house needs to breathe.

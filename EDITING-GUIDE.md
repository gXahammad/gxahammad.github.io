# Editing your website

Everything on your website — your name, projects, images, texts, CV, research and contact details — is
changed in the **website editor**. You never need to touch code. The design (fonts, layout, spacing) stays
the same whatever you add, so the site always looks consistent.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://gxahammad.github.io/admin/**
2. Click **Sign In Using Access Token**.
3. The first time only: click the link in the box to open GitHub (signed in as **gXahammad**). Under
   *Repository access* choose **Only select repositories → gxahammad.github.io**, check that *Contents* is
   set to *Read and write*, pick an expiry date, and click **Generate token**. Copy the long code, paste it
   into the box and click **Sign In**. Your browser remembers it. If you later see a sign-in error, the
   token has expired — make a new one the same way.

*(If your developer has set up "Sign In with GitHub", you'll see that button instead — just click it.)*

## How changes go live

- Press **Save** (top right) after editing. Your change is stored immediately.
- The website updates **1–3 minutes** later. Reload the page to see it.
- Every save is kept as a version, so nothing is ever lost. If something goes wrong, your developer can
  restore any earlier version.

## The editor at a glance

The left column has four sections, plus an **Assets** view (the picture icon at the top) with all your
images and files.

| Section | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects** | Your work — one entry per project |
| **Research & writing** | Papers, thesis, articles, essays, talks |
| **Pages** | The texts and images of the Home, Work, Research, About, Contact and "page not found" pages |
| **Site settings** | Your name, contact details, social links, link previews, the menu |

Every field has a short explanation underneath it. Fields marked **\*** are required; all others can be left
empty, and anything empty simply doesn't appear on the website.

## Projects

### Add a project

**Projects → New**, fill in the fields, **Save**. Or open an existing project and choose **⋮ → Duplicate**
to start from a copy with the same layout.

- **Project title**, **Year** (e.g. `2024` or `2022–2024`), **Location**, **Status** (e.g. *Built*, *Competition entry*).
- **Categories** — choose one or more. They become the filters on the Work page. To add or rename a
  category, go to **Pages → Work page → Project categories** first.
- **Short introduction** — one or two sentences, shown in large type at the top of the project page.
- **Main image** — the big picture at the top. Landscape works best.
- **Image description** — a few words describing the image, for people who can't see it and for Google.
- **Thumbnail image** *(optional)* — a different picture for the project grid, e.g. a sketch.
  **Thumbnail hover image** *(optional)* — appears when visitors point at the thumbnail.
- **Project details** — any extra facts: client, area, team, role, supervisor, award… Click **Add Detail**.
- **Description** — the main text. Use the toolbar for bold, italic, links and headings.
- **Images and content** — build the rest of the page from blocks. Click **Add Block** and choose:
  - **Image** — one picture, *edge to edge*, *page width* or *centred, smaller*, with a caption.
  - **Two images side by side**
  - **Image gallery** — many pictures in 2, 3 or 4 columns.
  - **Text** — a paragraph with an optional heading.
  - **Quote** — a sentence in large type.
  - **Video** — paste a YouTube or Vimeo link.

  Drag a block by its handle (or use its **⋮** menu) to move it up or down.
- **Links** — publications, award pages, competition boards…

Visitors can click any picture on a project page to see it full screen.

### Change the order of projects

New projects are added at the **end** of the list. Open **Projects → Reorder**, drag the projects into the
order you want, and save. The website shows them in exactly that order.

### Hide or delete a project

- To hide it but keep it: open the project, turn off **Show on website**, **Save**.
- To delete it: open it, **⋮** menu (top right) → **Delete**.

## Research & writing

**Research & writing → New.** Add the title, type (e.g. *Paper*, *Thesis*, *Essay* — these become the
filters), year, authors, where it was published, an abstract, a cover image, a **PDF file** and/or an
**external link** (journal page, DOI, ResearchGate…). Entries are listed by year, newest first.

## Pages

### Home page

The home page is a stack of **sections**. In **Pages → Home page → Sections** you can open, change, add
(**Add Section**), remove (**×**) or drag them into a new order:

- **Slideshow** — large rotating images. Each slide can **link to a project**: leave the slide's image and
  title empty to use the project's own. You can set the seconds per slide and the height.
- **Statement** — a short introduction in large type, with an optional link (e.g. text `About me`, address `/about`).
- **Selected projects** — choose which projects to show and in what order (leave empty for the first ones
  from your Work list), how many, and the layout.
- **Research & writing list** — your latest entries, or the ones you pick.
- **Large image**, **Text with image**, **Contact prompt**.

### Work page and Research page

Title, introduction, whether to show the filters, and — on the Work page — your **project categories** and
whether visitors first see the image grid or the text index. *"Words used on…"* lets you change every
small label (e.g. *Next project*, *Read the PDF*), for example to translate the site into Bangla.

### About page

Large introduction, **portrait**, **biography**, your **CV as a PDF** (a download button appears
automatically), **CV sections** (Education, Experience, Awards, Exhibitions… — add as many as you like, each
with entries) and **skills and languages**.

### Contact page

Heading, introduction and an optional image. Your email, phone, address and social links come from
**Site settings**, so they are the same everywhere on the site.

**Contact form (optional).** Create a free form at [formspree.io](https://formspree.io), copy the form
address (looks like `https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz`), paste it into **Contact form → Form address**, and
turn on **Show the contact form**. Messages arrive in your email.

## Site settings

- **Name, contact & social links** — your full name, role (e.g. *Architect*), where you're based, optional
  header text or logo image, email, phone, address, social links (LinkedIn, Instagram, Behance…), the
  description and picture used when your site is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or Facebook, the browser-tab
  icon, the footer text and copyright line, and general words such as *Menu* and *Back to top*.
- **Menu** — the links at the top of every page. Rename them, reorder them, remove one, or add a link to
  any other address (for example a portfolio PDF you uploaded, `/uploads/portfolio.pdf`).

## Tips for images and files

- Upload photos straight from your camera or phone — they are automatically converted and reduced to a
  web-friendly size (iPhone HEIC photos work too). Maximum 25 MB per file.
- Give files clear names before uploading (e.g. `courtyard-house-plan.jpg`); names become part of the address.
- Always fill in the **image description** fields.
- Drawings and sketches on a white background look great here — the whole site is white.
- To reuse an image you've already uploaded, click **Browse** in any image field and pick it from your assets.

## Tips for text

- **Enter** starts a new paragraph; **Shift + Enter** makes a line break inside a paragraph.
- Select text and press **Ctrl + B** (bold) or **Ctrl + I** (italic). The chain icon adds a link.
- Links to your own pages can be short, like `/work` or `/about`; other sites need the full address
  starting with `https://`.
